## Age gate

Wazoo is intended for people aged 13 and older. The Console asks you to confirm
that you are at least 13 years old before you create an account.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you
are under 13, do not create an account unless a parent or guardian has given
verifiable consent. If we learn that we have collected personal information from
a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.

## What we collect

- Account information you provide, such as your email address and display name.
- Usage data needed to operate the service, including world resources, API
  tokens, and daily write counts.
- Billing data if you subscribe to a paid plan. Stripe collects your payment
  details. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and your
  plan, and we do not receive or store your card number.
- Standard web analytics data. The wazoo.dev website uses Google Analytics.

## How we use information

- To operate and secure your account and worlds.
- To meter plan limits and manage platform usage.
- To process payments and manage your subscription.
- To send sign-in codes and other messages about your account.
- To respond to support requests and maintain service quality.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the service
providers required to operate the platform, and when required by law. These
providers act on our behalf:

- Cloudflare hosts the platform, the databases that hold your worlds, and the
  network that serves them.
- WorkOS handles sign-in and authentication.
- Resend delivers sign-in codes and account email.
- Stripe processes payments for paid plans.
- Google provides analytics for the wazoo.dev website.

Each provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Do Not Sell or Share

Wazoo does not sell or share your personal information for money or otherwise,
and has not done so in the preceding twelve months. You do not need to submit a
request to opt out, because there is nothing to opt out of. If this changes in
the future, we will update this policy and honor Global Privacy Control signals.

## Your choices

You can export your account data and delete your account from Settings in the
Console. Deleting your account removes your account information, cancels any
subscription, and marks your worlds for deletion. Worlds are permanently purged
after a 30-day grace period. Stripe keeps billing records, such as invoices and
the customer record, as it is required to for tax and accounting purposes.

If you cannot use the Console, contact the team at
[wazoo.dev/meet](https://wazoo.dev/meet). See the
[Worlds data API](/worlds/query) for tools to export and manage the data in your
worlds.
