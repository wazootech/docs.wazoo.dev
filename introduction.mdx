Wazoo is an open-source verifiable context engine built to manage, query, and
prove interoperable knowledge bases at the edge.

It provides agents with a deterministic context graph that offers complete
explainability and source provenance—ensuring traceable reasoning and
continually improving output quality without relying on uninspectable
vector-only memory.
