Wazoo is the open-source verifiable context engine designed to manage, query,
and prove interoperable knowledge bases at the edge.

It provides agents with a deterministic context graph that offers complete
explainability and source provenance—ensuring continually improving output
quality and traceable reasoning without relying on uninspectable vector-only
memory.
