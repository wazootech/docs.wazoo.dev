Wazoo is an open-source verifiable context engine designed to manage, query, and
prove interoperable knowledge bases at the edge.

Instead of relying on fuzzy text blobs or uninspectable vector-only memory,
Wazoo gives agents a deterministic context graph that offers complete
explainability and source provenance—ensuring traceable reasoning and
continually improving output quality.

## The Wazoo platform & ecosystem

Wazoo provides the core context platform alongside an ecosystem of standalone
open-source projects that share the same standards, patterns, and semantic
model:

## Architectural pillars

- **Verifiable world models**: Give agents a durable, auditable substrate for
  facts, relationships, and decision provenance.
- **Semantic Markdown**: Allow human-authored Markdown documents to participate
  natively in graph workflows.
- **Provider & LLM agnostic**: Strictly provider-agnostic infrastructure.
  Connect your own LLM, embedding models, and memory stores.
- **Edge-native & local-first**: Designed for fast, low-latency execution in
  microkernel and serverless environments.
