## Plans and limits

The Wazoo Platform has a Free plan and a paid Pro plan. Each plan sets a daily
limit on the rows your worlds write and a cap on the number of worlds you can
hold. When you reach the daily limit, writes are refused with an error until the
next UTC day. Reads are never refused, and we do not delete your data because
you reached a limit. We may change plan limits, and we will say so in the
service before a change takes effect. Your plan and your usage against the daily
limit are shown in Settings in the Console.
